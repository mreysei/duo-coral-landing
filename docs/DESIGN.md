## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a refined Mediterranean and Macaronesian coastal luxury aesthetic, calibrated specifically for high-end hospitality decision-makers: General Managers, Luxury Wedding Planners, and Food & Beverage Directors of 5-star resorts across Tenerife. 

The visual style blends **Minimalism** with subtle **Glassmorphic** and **Luminescent Coastal** nuances. It avoids nautical clichés in favor of sunlit sea depths, glowing sunset coral reflections, and architectural restraint. Space is treated with generous cadence, high typographic poise, and quiet opulence. The emotional resonance is calm, prestigious, deeply musical, and flawlessly executed—evoking warm evening trade winds, acoustic elegance against Atlantic waters, and peerless service standards.

## Colors

The palette captures twilight on the Canary coastline: deep Atlantic azure contrasted with luminous living coral, underpinned by warm sand and brushed champagne accents.

- **Primary (`#0D3B66` - Atlantic Azure):** The commanding, regal ocean anchor. Used for principal brand presence, formal headings, high-priority buttons, and dominant visual weight.
- **Secondary (`#FF6F61` - Glowing Salmon Coral):** The vibrant lifeblood of the identity. Used for key conversion moments, curated music tags, interactive hover states, and warm emotional focal points.
- **Tertiary (`#D4AF37` - Champagne Gold):** A subtle acoustic metal accent. Applied sparingly to badges, VIP indicators, star ratings, and delicate hairline separators.
- **Neutral (`#121F2C` - Deep Sea Slate):** High-contrast base for body typography and deep interface grounds, avoiding stark pure black in favor of deep oceanic graphite.

### Surface System
- `surface-canvas`: `#FAF8F5` (Warm Canary sand off-white)
- `surface-elevated`: `#FFFFFF` (Pure pristine white)
- `surface-tinted`: `#FFF1EE` (Light coral cream tint for highlighted selections, quotes, and VIP cards)
- `border-subtle`: `rgba(13, 59, 102, 0.08)` (Muted sea-line hairpins)
- `border-gold`: `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)` (Delicate decorative framing)

## Typography

The typography couples the editorial romance of **Playfair Display** with the crisp, modern legibility of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

- **Playfair Display** commands titles, repertoire genres, and luxury narratives. It brings classical musical pedigree and Mediterranean charm. It should be typeset with measured line-heights and intentional letter-spacing to let its high stroke contrast breathe.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** delivers functional clarity across hospitality specs, equipment riders, schedules, and interface controls.
- **Micro-labels and Overlines:** Set in `label-lg` or `label-md` with uppercase styling and expanded tracking (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) to echo luxury horology and high-end hotel collateral.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a 12-column fluid grid on desktop, shifting to an 8-column layout on tablet, and a 4-column structure on mobile devices.

- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, `4rem` outer canvas margins, with maximum content width capped at `1280px` for optimal reading comfort.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, `2rem` margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` gutters, `1.25rem` margins.

The spacing rhythm emphasizes asymmetric, editorial pacing. Vertical gaps between narrative sections (`space-2xl` and above) remain deliberately generous to reflect five-star serenity, ensuring no element feels crowded.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses tonal surface layering and coastal atmospheric diffusion rather than heavy physical drops.

- **Level 0 (Base):** Flat `#FAF8F5` surface with hairline dividers (`rgba(13, 59, 102, 0.08)`).
- **Level 1 (Cards & Modules):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface accompanied by a soft, warm ocean shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(13, 59, 102, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(13, 59, 102, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Overlays & Floating Audio Players):** Subtle translucent backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` on `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)`), accented by a deeper tinted ambient shadow: `0 12px 36px -4px rgba(13, 59, 102, 0.10), 0 2px 8px -1px rgba(255, 111, 97, 0.06)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs & Key Booking Drawers):** High-diffusion elevation: `0 24px 64px -12px rgba(13, 59, 102, 0.22)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Soft (1)** shape language. Architectural precision is paramount: corners carry a slight, restrained radius (`4px` / `0.25rem` default) rather than bubbly or toy-like curvatures.

- Small buttons, badges, and input elements use `0.25rem` (`4px`).
- Media cards and interactive content panels use `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / `8px`).
- Floating audio controls or pill tags may use circular geometries strictly where tactile affordance demands a gesture toward musical instruments and playback knobs.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Atlantic Azure):** Solid `#0D3B66` background, crisp white typography, `4px` corner radius. Interactive state shifts smoothly into `#082642` with an ambient sea-tinted shadow.
- **Secondary (Coral Glow):** Solid `#FF6F61` background, white typography. Used for instant bookings, inquiries, and listening cues. Hover shifts to `#E05344`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Border `1px solid rgba(13, 59, 102, 0.2)` with `#0D3B66` text, transitioning on hover to `#FAF8F5` with subtle champagne border `#D4AF37`.

### Cards
- **Repertoire & Performance Cards:** Rendered on `#FFFFFF` with a crisp `1px` border of `rgba(13, 59, 102, 0.06)`. Feature image ratios in 4:5 or 16:9 with subtle contrast overlays to hold white typography. Hover elevates the card by `-2px` with expanded warm shadow.
- **VIP Package Cards:** Finished with an interior padding of `space-xl`, top hairline highlight in `#D4AF37`, and soft `#FFF1EE` tinted badge.

### Audio Player & Sound Bites
- Custom floating bar utilizing glassmorphic `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)` blur, brushed champagne wave scrubbing bar, and coral `#FF6F61` play/pause action button. Track labels use `label-md` uppercase styling.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background: `#FFFFFF`. Border: `1px solid rgba(13, 59, 102, 0.16)`. Focus state applies a crisp `1px solid #0D3B66` with a delicate `3px` focus ring tinted in `rgba(255, 111, 97, 0.18)`. Placeholder text in muted slate (`#6E8294`).

### Chips & Tags
- Repertoire chips (e.g., *Bossa Nova*, *Acoustic Pop*, *Sunset Lounge*): `#FFF1EE` background, `#0D3B66` text, padding `space-xs space-md`, with an understated `1px solid rgba(255, 111, 97, 0.2)` border.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square / circular controls with a `1.5px` border in `#0D3B66`. Checked states fill with `#0D3B66` displaying a pure white checkmark or inner ring.
